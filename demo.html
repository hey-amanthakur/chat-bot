Embed the Chat Widget

Add this single script tag to any website:

<script
  src="http://localhost:3000/widgets/chat-widget.min.js"
  data-client-id="dr-smith-dental"
  data-api-url="http://localhost:3000"
  data-color="#2563eb"
  data-icon="🦷"
  data-header="Talk to Dr. Smith"
  data-position="bottom-right"
  data-greeting="Hi! Welcome to Dr. Smith Dental."
></script>
